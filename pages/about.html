Our Story

It all started in a small kitchen with a dream to create the creamiest, most flavorful ice creams using fresh ingredients. What began as a family tradition is now a place where everyone comes to enjoy sweet, unforgettable moments.

As word spread, our passion grew into a beloved community favorite. We opened our first shop with one belief—ice cream is more than dessert; it’s a way to bring people together and create lasting memories.

At Sweet Treats, every flavor tells a story—whether it’s a classic vanilla bean, rich chocolate fudge, or a unique seasonal delight. Today, our shop is a place where joy is served, one scoop at a time. Come visit us and be part of our sweet journey! 🍨
